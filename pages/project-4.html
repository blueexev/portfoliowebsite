Project Iti

"How might we turn Indian folktales into an interactive experience where children don't just hear the story — they build, shape and perform it?"

Role Concept / Product Design / Fabrication
Timeline Month Year – Month Year
Tools AR / Physical Prototyping / Illustration
Link Project link ↗

01 / Context

Context

Inspired by India's deep-rooted storytelling traditions — ranging from folk theatre and puppetry to oral narratives passed down through generations — I created Iti as a way to help children reconnect with this rich cultural heritage in a contemporary format. India is known for its diverse traditional art forms like puppetry, folklore and regional storytelling practices, but many of these are slowly fading in today's digital age.

02 / The problem

The problem

Children often experience stories by simply listening or watching. I wanted to explore how storytelling could become something they build, touch, change and perform themselves.

03 / The game

The game

Iti is an interactive storytelling game and DIY theatre kit designed to help children rediscover India's rich cultural roots through folktales and performance. Blending craft, play and technology, Iti encourages young minds to create, imagine and connect with traditional stories in a modern way.

Each child becomes both a creator and performer — building their own miniature puppet theatre, crafting characters, exploring them in 3D through AR, and finally bringing the tale to life through a dramatic act. An added AR layer lets them bring their handmade puppets to life in 3D, blending traditional storytelling with digital wonder.

04 / Inspiration

Inspiration

Drawing from traditional Indian puppet theatre — especially shadow puppetry and string puppetry — the mood is rooted in drama, folklore and handcrafted aesthetics. The stage compositions, rich textiles and ornamental frames reflect the essence of live folk performances, where storytelling unfolds through movement, light and silhouette.

05 / Experience of the gameplay

Experience of the gameplay

Make

Children build their personal mini theatre using pre-cut materials from the kit.

Create

They design and decorate character puppets inspired by folktales.

Discover

Using AR, they scan activation cards to explore their characters coming to life.

Perform

With dialogue flashcards, they enact the story as a full theatre act — learning moral lessons, emotions and cultural nuances of our country through play and game.

06 / What is inside the kit?

What is inside the kit?

  • DIY Theatre Kit — pre-cut materials and assembly guides to build a personal mini stage.
  • Character Templates — blank puppets and cutouts to design, decorate and personalize.
  • Cultural Insight Cards — fun facts and short learning prompts about regions and folklore origins.
  • Story Flashcards — traditional Indian folktales broken down into acts and dialogues.
  • AR Activation Cards — scan to view your characters come alive in 3D through an AR feature.
  • Instruction Booklet — step-by-step guide for setup and performance.

07 / Making storytelling interactive

Making storytelling interactive

Instead of giving children one fixed way to tell the story, Iti gives them pieces to experiment with. They can choose characters, arrange the stage, use dialogue cards and perform the story in their own way.

The AR layer adds another dimension to the physical play. Children can scan elements of the kit to discover additional parts of the story, making the physical theatre feel like it has another hidden layer.

Iti turns storytelling into a shared act of making and performing. Children build something together, give characters a voice and become part of the story instead of simply watching it.
