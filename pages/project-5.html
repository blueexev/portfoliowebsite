Digital Museum of Tamil Culture

An immersive interactive museum exploring Tamil Nadu's stories, art, architecture and mythology.

01 / Project overview

Project overview

This project explores how digital spaces can make cultural heritage more interactive and engaging. I designed and built a virtual museum in Unity that brings together different aspects of Tamil culture — from mythology and traditional art to architecture and sculpture.

Rather than presenting culture as static information, I created an environment where visitors can explore, interact and discover stories through play.

02 / The experience

The experience

The museum is organised as a spatial journey through different elements of Tamil heritage. Visitors move through the environment, discovering cultural objects, architectural references and interactive experiences. It includes:

Tanjore Painting

Traditional Tamil painting and its visual richness.

Nataraja

The symbolism and cultural significance of the dancing Shiva.

M. S. Subbulakshmi

Celebrating the legacy of the iconic Carnatic vocalist and her contribution to Tamil and Indian musical heritage.

Silappathikaram

Bringing one of Tamil literature's most significant epics to life through an interactive storytelling experience.

Gopuram

Exploring the monumental temple towers that define Dravidian architecture and Tamil temple landscapes.

03 / The games

The games

Silappathikaram Sliding Puzzle

An interactive game where solving the puzzle reveals the story of Kannagi and Kovalan.

Towering Stones Game

A puzzle-based interaction exploring the belief that towering stones symbolise keeping families together.

04 / Design approach

Design approach

I focused on creating a space that feels like a digital cultural landscape rather than a conventional museum. Spatial layout, scale, movement, interaction and visual elements were used to guide visitors through the experience.

The project allowed me to explore how play can become a method of cultural storytelling — making heritage something that can be experienced and discovered rather than simply observed.

The spatial layout

08 / Learnings

Learnings

Spatial storytelling

Understood how layout, movement, scale and environment can guide a user through a narrative.

Game-based storytelling

Explored how simple mechanics like puzzles can make cultural narratives more engaging and memorable.

Cultural research

Learned how to translate cultural stories and traditions into engaging digital experiences.

Unity & 3D design

Gained experience in building interactive spaces, designing environments and creating immersive experiences in Unity.
