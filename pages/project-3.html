Selected Work

Fragile Flights

A data-driven visualisation exploring how poor air quality — especially PM2.5 pollution from urban construction — affects bird health and habitat connectivity across Ahmedabad.

Medium Data Visualisation / Simulation
Role Research / Interaction / Visual
Tools p5.js
Year 2025

01 / Project brief

Project brief

Ahmedabad today exists as a collection of fragmented ecological islands. Famous green lungs like Parimal Garden and the Riverfront are majestic in isolation, but they are severed by vast, hostile expanses of heat and toxic air.

While our urban planning has successfully created a seamless network for human transit and concrete density, it has inadvertently constructed a series of "Biological Deserts" for the wildlife that sustains our ecosystem.

For a bird or a pollinator, the city is not a grid of roads; it is a landscape of impassable "Chemical Walls," where high AQI and extreme thermal stress act as invisible barriers, trapping life in shrinking pockets of green.

02 / Intent

Intent

This project focuses on birds in Ahmedabad. Through data visualisation, it highlights how poor air quality especially PM2.5 pollution caused by urban construction affects bird health and disrupts their natural habitat and survival.

03 / Framing

Why birds? Why architects?

Two influence impact matrices were used to position birds and architects on the same axes most/least influential against most/least affected to make the case for why each belongs at the centre of this project.

04 / Target audience

Target audience

Architects

This data prompts architects and urban planners to advocate for inserting green bubbles every 500 metres as an ecological requirement. Our target audience are the designers of the city who can make an actual impact.

  • To move away from anthropocentric design, the shift must happen at the planning level.
  • Planners are the ones who can transform Ahmedabad from a series of disconnected parks into a breathable, living network.
  • They are the professionals responsible for essential life-support infrastructure this project argues that biological connectivity is a fundamental part of that urban utility.

05 / Datasets

Datasets we chose to work with

  • The physical map of the city of Ahmedabad
  • The AQI data over the past 3 years (2022–2024)
  • The body mass of specific bird species
  • Locations of different talukas and important places, in (x, y) format
  • AQI data across the city most and least affected
  • Research papers

06 / Major components

Major components of our project

Loading Screen

User selects a bird species, and the corresponding habitat and AQI map load.

Bird Simulation

Birds move as a flock using noise-based motion, cohesion behavior and collision avoidance.

AQI Detection

Each bird samples AQI map colors. Pollution severity is classified into different colours.

Health System

Three sliders decrease based on pollution:
Lung Damage
Wing Damage
Neurological Damage

Path Visualization

After extinction:
Bird movement paths can be displayed.
User can export path image.
User can restart.

Weekly Progression

Every 10 seconds = 1 week.
Environmental warnings appear.
Birds gradually die over time.

07 / Goal

Goal of the project

The goal is to generate a map that identifies connectivity gaps — places where wildlife is being throttled.

More broadly, the project is meant to prompt urban architects and planners to create ecological spaces and bubbles for birds to cope with bad AQI levels. It also adds value if people become more aware of their own actions and their effect.

08 / Visual language

Visual language of the project

A high-contrast palette of alert reds, deep blues and toxic greens was used throughout the interface to keep the tone urgent and diagnostic, closer to a warning system than a nature app.
