01 — About

A little about me.

The person behind the pixels, prototypes and slightly too many open tabs.

Your photo goes here

Hi, I'm Vaishnavi. I'm an interaction design student interested in the space between technology, culture and storytelling.

My work moves between UI/UX, visual design, AR/VR, immersive experiences and digital storytelling. I enjoy taking something that initially feels abstract and finding a way for people to experience it rather than simply look at it.

I'm especially drawn to projects involving heritage, museums, Indian visual culture and emerging technology. But I'm equally happy experimenting with a tiny interaction just because I wondered what would happen if I clicked it.
